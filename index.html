I Cook for the Guest

My background spans high-end hotels, chef-driven tasting-menu restaurants, and private yachts—including serving on motor yacht Mia Copa, one of the largest sport fishers in the world. Experience includes managing remote provisioning from Alaska down to Costa Rica on the West Coast, as well as across the East Coast from Newfoundland and the St. Lawrence down to the deepest Caribbean. This background provides a wide repertoire of culinary execution rooted in classical technique while remaining fully in touch with current trends.

Accustomed to shifting conditions and unscripted operational demands, I deliver refined, approachable food that nods to the classics without being precious—including adapting to specific culinary styles from a guest's favorite restaurants. I handle specialized requirements—including gluten-free, dairy-free, and specific dietary needs—seamlessly, maintaining full flavor and execution regardless of constraints.

Formal culinary training at the Culinary Institute of Canada combined with nearly two decades of high-level professional cooking across luxury hotels, private estates, and yachts. Operational mindset grounded in sea time, strict food safety controls, and clean execution under pressure.
